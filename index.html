What this is not

A feed, a creator platform, a dating app, or a continuous-recording product. There are no profiles to browse, no follower counts, no stage, and no DMs. Audio leaves the device only when someone files a report — and then only the last 30 seconds.

The two hard stops

Any confirmed minor in an 18+ room stops matching immediately and triggers an external audit. Day 90 with DAU below 800 and D7 below 20% stops the consumer product; the remaining options are a cohort/B2B2C pivot or returning capital. Both are written down in advance, which is the point.
