Eloq — an AI English coach that measures you

An always-available speaking coach for people with a dated, high-stakes English event: a job interview, a first job, a semester abroad. Phoneme-level feedback, an error-memory review queue, and a weekly measure of whether you are actually getting better.

Positioning: the reliable version of language exchange. HelloTalk is 70M users whose supply is volunteers' free time; we sell the one thing it cannot promise — evidence that you are improving.

What this is not

There is no app binary yet. This set is the plan, the model, and the falsification tests — deliberately written before the build so the expensive mistakes happen in spreadsheets and $2.5k landing pages instead of in production. The model is reproducible: build_model.py regenerates every CSV and every scenario number quoted here.
