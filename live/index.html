v5 pivots the core object from the person to the room. 01–08 document the language-exchange product and remain as the record. 09-v5-master-plan.md supersedes their pricing and room model: 15-minute topic rooms, three to six people, subscription plus two consumables. Read 09 first if you are looking for the current plan.

One line: tap once, get a room worth fifteen minutes with three to five other people — and no way for anyone to message you afterwards.

What this is: the design, build, and monetization plan for a live-audio network of small, timeboxed, high-signal rooms — HelloTalk's premise (real people, no cold text) rebuilt with Clubhouse's spontaneity and neither product's failure modes.

What this is not: the AI-tutor plan in ../eloq/. That set optimises for phoneme scoring and interview content sold as a subscription to one learner. This is a network where the product is the room, not the model. See §0 for what carries over.

The whole product in six sentences

  1. You open the app and tap one topic.
  2. It puts you in a room with three to five people who chose the same one.
  3. You get fifteen minutes, a prompt if you want one, and a balance meter.
  4. A quiet hairline shows the time; the controls disappear after six seconds.
  5. If someone is a problem, one long-press removes them and the room continues.
  6. At the end you get three takeaways and one question to come back to.

No profiles to browse. No follower counts. No stage. No DMs. No ghosting — if no human room is open, an AI co-host can take the third seat, and it says so.

0. What carries over from the AI-tutor plan, and what dies

Item Verdict
Safety-first architecture (age gate, no location, no identifiers) Carries over, and becomes the core differentiator
Pricing anchors: HelloTalk VIP verified at $12.99/mo · $79.99/yr (2026-11-15) Carries over — parity is the argument
Evidence discipline: every number tagged (V)/(D)/(H) with a test Carries over
Interview-prep wedge, career rooms Carries over as a paid tier, not the whole product
Pronunciation engine, phoneme scoring, speech model licensing Dies. Not the product. Corrections here are social, not acoustic.
The 4-persona doc set, the 25-invariant financial model, the pronunciation validation protocol Dies as core. The model can be rebuilt from the live-audio cost base in 05.
"No stranger chat, ever" Dies. This product is stranger voice. The entire safety design exists because of that.

If both sets are ever shown to the same person, this one is the product and the other is a feature (Career rooms with feedback).

The three decisions this design locks

# Decision Why it is locked
1 18+ at launch, 16–17 only after a specific safety review Stranger voice chat with minors is the single fastest way to end the company. HelloTalk's 13+ rating is their weakness; it is not a benchmark to copy.
2 Reciprocal matching only for 1:1 (you help them, they help you) One-way "talk to a native" is a charity queue that empties. Reciprocity is what keeps supply alive at 3 a.m.
3 One primary action per screen This is the anti-Clubhouse rule. Any screen that needs a second decision goes back to design.

What would make me kill this

Written before the build, so it cannot be rationalised later:

  • Median time-to-human-match stays above 45 seconds after 8 weeks of seed liquidity → the "instant" promise is false; narrow to one topic cluster or move to scheduled tables.
  • Report rate above 3 per 1,000 room-minutes with no downward trend → the safety model is not working; freeze growth.
  • D7 below 20% at week 6 of beta → live audio is novelty, not a habit; re-scope to goal-based practice rooms with the same safety layer.
  • Any confirmed CSAM or sexual-conduct incident involving a minor → stop matching entirely, external audit, no exceptions.

Each of these is expanded in 07-risks.md with the metric, the threshold, and the action.
