Rule for this document: if a screen has two primary actions, it is wrong. Every screen below has exactly one thing to press.

1. The four flows

1.1 First run (launch target: 60 seconds to first audio)

Welcome ──► Age gate ──► Your language ──► Practise language ──► Level ──► Verify ──► Mic check ──► Home ──► Matching
  10s          5s            10s                10s              20s        20s         10s          —        —
Step What the user does What the app does Why it exists
Welcome Nothing — one button: Get started Explains the promise in one sentence No carousel, no login wall
Age gate Taps "I'm 18 or older" Blocks under-18s with a clear reason Legal + safety; no dark pattern
Your language Taps one chip Sets L1 —
Practise language Taps one chip Sets L2 and pair Only pairs with live supply are shown; the two rows can never hold the same language
Level Speaks for 20 seconds, or skips Estimates A1–C1 as a matching band, not a grade; costs nothing to skip Self-report alone is noisy; audio alone is scary
Verify Enters a phone number, receives one code Sends an OTP, binds the number to the device (hashed); no name, photo, or email Every room has a reachable adult behind it
Mic check Says one sentence Plays it back, checks level and echo 40% of first-call failures are audio setup
Home Taps Talk Enters queue —

No profile before value. There is no password, email, bio, or photo before the first room. A phone number, verified in about 20 seconds, is the only gate — and it is never shown to anyone. Apple/Google sign-in after the first room is optional and only saves progress across devices. The selfie liveness check comes before the third room, once the person has a reason to stay (04 §3).

Launch variant (v1). The six screens below are the full set. The launch path drops the level screen (it moves after room 1 and is inferred from the first call) and folds the mic check into matching, so the tap path is Welcome → Age → Language → Phone → Home → Talk — five taps plus a phone number and a code (08 §4). The full flow remains the reference for what the product does; the launch funnel is what gets a stranger into a room in under a minute.

Level is not a test. The estimate only sets the matching band (±1 level), so being one band off is harmless. There is no stepper, no score, and no retake; it can be changed later in Settings.

1.2 Matching

Tap Talk
  └─► Finding (0–10s) ──────────────► Human room
        │
        └─ at 10s: "No one free right now" (inline, never a modal)
              ├─► Keep waiting for a person ──► human room in ~2s
              ├─► Practise with AI while waiting ──► AI room; human handover in < 3s
              └─► Cancel ──► Home
State UI Copy
Finding One pulsing ring, pair chips, elapsed seconds not shown as a countdown "Finding someone who wants to practise English"
Match found Ring snaps to solid, partner's display name + level + topic "Mateo · B1 · Interview practice" → auto-join after 2s
Fallback Same layout, label changes "No one free — Ana can practise with you for now"
Handover Partner card slides in mid-call "Mateo is free — handing over in 3s" · Stay with Ana

Why the 10-second rule: a queue that feels infinite reads as broken. Ten seconds is long enough for a real match and short enough to believe. The AI fallback means the queue can never produce dead time — but the person must always be told which one they are talking to.

Matching inputs, in priority order: language pair → level band (±1) → topic → recent partner history (never repeat the same person twice in 24h unless both opt in) → reciprocity balance.

Correction cue (the differentiator that must stay light): 1. Tap the "…" → Correct my Spanish (the row names the language you are learning). 2. Your last sentence is captured silently in the background. Nothing is repeated back to you first. 3. About a second later one cue arrives, inline, above the controls: Try: "I've been working there since 2020." — with You said: "I work there since 2020." underneath. 4. Tone rules: the label says Correction, never "wrong"; the accent is warm; one cue at a time; no score, no replay button, no lesson. 5. Tap the cue, press Enter, or wait six seconds to dismiss. Hovering or focusing it holds it open. 6. The corrected phrase is kept for the recap's word bank. The cue never interrupts the conversation and never opens a sheet.

Translation (gloss): live captions are off by default. When they are on, tap a word in the caption strip → one-word gloss, 2-second display, then gone. There is no sentence translation and no "translate" action in the room menu — reading replaces speaking.

1.4 Group table (3–6)

Join table ──► 3-min round ──► rotate speaker ──► 3-min round ──► … ──► recap
  • No host. A rotating "table lead" is chosen per round — the person who has spoken least.
  • Round timer is visible to everyone; at 0:00 the lead picks the next speaker or the app picks the person with the least time.
  • Raise-hand-free: you tap your own circle to indicate "I'd like the next turn"; no queue management UI.
  • Pass is one tap and carries no penalty.
  • Hard cap: 6. Above that, someone is always silent, and silence is what killed Clubhouse rooms.

1.5 Recap and the learning loop

Leave room ──► Recap (30s max) ──► Home ──► optional nudge next day
Recap block Content Interaction
You spoke 6:40 of 10:00 · 41 utterances None — just the number
Words you reached for 3 words you paused on or asked for Tap to save to word bank
Two fixes The corrections you flagged, one line each Tap to hear the model version
One next step "Next time: practise past tense in interview answers" Accept → becomes tomorrow's prompt
Save / Skip Never auto-posted, never shared, no confetti Two buttons, equal weight

Streak model: a weekly goal (3 sessions), not a daily streak. Missing a day does not reset anything. The nudge is at a user-chosen time, once, and it can be turned off in one tap.

3. Edge cases that decide whether this feels trustworthy

Case Behaviour
Partner leaves mid-sentence AI takes over in under 2s with the same topic and timer; "Talk again" is one tap; no penalty, no requeue wait, no explanation required
You lose connection Auto-reconnect for 30s; after that, a one-tap "rejoin or leave"
Partner is silent for 60s Prompt appears for you only: "Ask them a question" — never a public callout
Someone is inappropriate One tap on Report ends the call for both, mutes you instantly, opens the report sheet; the other side sees "Call ended", not "You were reported"
You are reported You see "Call ended" and nothing else; enforcement is asynchronous and appealable (04 §6)
No one in your pair App tells you the truth ("only 3 people online for Japanese right now") and offers the nearest pair with supply
Blocked user re-queues Matching excludes blocked pairs permanently, device-level

Anti-dark-pattern rules: no "are you sure you want to leave?", no streak-loss warnings, no artificial scarcity, no "someone is waiting for you" notifications that are not true.

4. Navigation model

Three destinations, not five:

Home ──Talk──► Room ──leave/report──► Recap ──Done──► Home
  └─ Settings (gear; never during a call)

Settings is a leaf, not a tab. Nothing in the map is a feed and nothing is public.

No tab bar during a call. No persistent bottom nav. The back gesture always means "leave the room", and it always asks nothing.
