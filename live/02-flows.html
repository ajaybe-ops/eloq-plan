Rule for this document: if a screen has two primary actions, it is wrong. Every screen below has exactly one thing to press.

1. The four flows

1.1 First run (target: 75 seconds to first room)

Welcome ──► Age gate ──► Your language ──► Practise language ──► Level ──► Intent ──► Mic check ──► Home ──► Matching
  10s          5s            10s                10s              20s         10s         10s          —        —
Step What the user does What the app does Why it exists
Welcome Nothing — one button: Get started Explains the promise in one sentence No carousel, no login wall
Age gate Taps "I'm 18 or older" Blocks under-18s with a clear reason Legal + safety; no dark pattern
Your language Taps one chip Sets L1 —
Practise language Taps one chip Sets L2 and pair Only pairs with live supply are shown
Level Speaks for 20 seconds Estimates A1–C1, shows the estimate and lets them adjust one notch Self-report alone is noisy; audio alone is scary
Intent Taps one chip (Interview / Everyday / Exam / Just talking) Sets topic defaults Drives room content
Mic check Says one sentence Plays it back, checks level and echo 40% of first-call failures are audio setup
Home Taps Talk Enters queue —

No account creation before value. Sign-in (Apple/Google/email) happens after the first room, framed as "save your progress". Phone verification is required only before the second room — that is the point where a bad actor costs someone real time.

1.2 Matching

Tap Talk
  └─► Finding (0–12s) ──────────────► Room
        │
        ├─ at 12s: "No one free right now"
        │     ├─► Practise with AI while waiting ──► AI room; human handover when available
        │     └─► Change topic / language ──► back to Finding
        └─ at 45s: offer to join a table or leave the queue
State UI Copy
Finding One pulsing ring, pair chips, elapsed seconds not shown as a countdown "Finding someone who wants to practise English"
Match found Ring snaps to solid, partner's display name + level + topic "Mateo · B1 · Interview practice" → auto-join after 2s
Fallback Same layout, label changes "No one free — Ana can practise with you for now"
Handover Partner card slides in mid-call "A person just arrived. Switch? 10s" — never forced

Why the 12-second rule: a queue that feels infinite reads as broken. Twelve seconds is long enough for a real match and short enough to believe. The AI fallback means the queue can never produce dead time — but the person must always be told which one they are talking to.

Matching inputs, in priority order: language pair → level band (±1) → intent/topic → recent partner history (never repeat the same person twice in 24h unless both opt in) → reciprocity balance.

Element Behaviour Why
Pair + timer Top-left, small, monospaced digits Orientation without a dashboard
Connection dot Green / amber / red; tap for a one-line diagnosis Audio problems are the #1 silent churn cause
Presence Two circles that gently expand while that person talks You can see who is speaking without a name-tag hierarchy
Balance meter One bar, two segments; amber if one side exceeds 75% for 90 seconds The only nudge toward pedagogy that is not a lesson
Prompt card Collapsed after 60s; pull up to change Structure for people who freeze; invisible for people who don't
Leave Always visible, never confirmed with a guilt screen Trust
Mute 72px, centre, unmistakable state The one control that must never be missed
More Correct cue, translate a word, report, block Destructive actions are one level deeper, never in the thumb's path

Correction cue (the differentiator that must stay light): 1. Long-press the "…" → Correct something. 2. The last 10 seconds of transcript appear as tappable phrases. 3. Tap the phrase, type or accept a suggested version. 4. Partner sees a small chip: "Try: 'I've been working there since 2020'" — no interruption, no modal, dismissible. 5. Chip is saved to their recap automatically.

Translation: tap a word in the live caption strip → one-word gloss, 2-second display, then gone. No full-sentence translation — that turns practice into reading.

1.4 Group table (3–6)

Join table ──► 3-min round ──► rotate speaker ──► 3-min round ──► … ──► recap
  • No host. A rotating "table lead" is chosen per round — the person who has spoken least.
  • Round timer is visible to everyone; at 0:00 the lead picks the next speaker or the app picks the person with the least time.
  • Raise-hand-free: you tap your own circle to indicate "I'd like the next turn"; no queue management UI.
  • Pass is one tap and carries no penalty.
  • Hard cap: 6. Above that, someone is always silent, and silence is what killed Clubhouse rooms.

1.5 Recap and the learning loop

Leave room ──► Recap (30s max) ──► Home ──► optional nudge next day
Recap block Content Interaction
You spoke 6:40 of 10:00 · 41 utterances None — just the number
Words you reached for 3 words you paused on or asked for Tap to save to word bank
Two fixes The corrections you flagged, one line each Tap to hear the model version
One next step "Next time: practise past tense in interview answers" Accept → becomes tomorrow's prompt
Save / Skip Never auto-posted, never shared, no confetti Two buttons, equal weight

Streak model: a weekly goal (3 sessions), not a daily streak. Missing a day does not reset anything. The nudge is at a user-chosen time, once, and it can be turned off in one tap.

Settings, profile, word bank, and billing exist but are not part of the core flow; they are reached from a single gear on Home and never interrupt a session.

3. Edge cases that decide whether this feels trustworthy

Case Behaviour
Partner leaves mid-sentence 5-second "Mateo left" state → immediate requeue with the same topic, no penalty, no explanation required
You lose connection Auto-reconnect for 30s; after that, a one-tap "rejoin or leave"
Partner is silent for 60s Prompt appears for you only: "Ask them a question" — never a public callout
Someone is inappropriate One tap on Report ends the call for both, mutes you instantly, opens the report sheet; the other side sees "Call ended", not "You were reported"
You are reported You see "Call ended" and nothing else; enforcement is asynchronous and appealable (04 §6)
No one in your pair App tells you the truth ("only 3 people online for Japanese right now") and offers the nearest pair with supply
Blocked user re-queues Matching excludes blocked pairs permanently, device-level

Anti-dark-pattern rules: no "are you sure you want to leave?", no streak-loss warnings, no artificial scarcity, no "someone is waiting for you" notifications that are not true.

4. Navigation model

Three destinations, not five:

                 ┌──────────────┐
                 │    Home      │  ← Talk, table, online count
                 └──────┬───────┘
        ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
     Recap           Room            Settings
   (from a call)   (modal, full)    (gear, top-right)

No tab bar during a call. No persistent bottom nav. The back gesture always means "leave the room", and it always asks nothing.
