What to notice

One action per screen. Every step has a single filled button. The ghost options are secondary and never compete.

No hierarchy. Two equal presence circles. No host, no audience, no badges.

The balance bar is the only pedagogical nudge — it turns amber if one person speaks for 90 seconds.

Corrections arrive as a chip, not a modal. They never interrupt the call.

Recap is private and takes 30 seconds. No confetti, no share prompt.
