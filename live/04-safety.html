The uncomfortable premise: this product asks strangers to speak to each other with their voices. Voice is intimate, hard to moderate, and impossible to un-hear. HelloTalk's biggest weakness is exactly here — 13+ age rating, open messaging, location collection, and profiles that invite the wrong attention. Copying their posture would import their problem.

Safety is therefore a product feature with its own budget, headcount, and launch gates — not a policy page.

1. Threat model

Threat Who Severity Why it matters here
Sexual harassment / exposure Predatory adults Existential Live voice, no text trail, victim leaves and never returns
Minors in adult rooms 13–17 Existential One incident ends the company and possibly someone's safety
Doxxing / off-platform contact Anyone High Voice reveals accent, name, sometimes workplace
Scams / romance fraud Organised High "Practise English" is a plausible opening
Hate speech, slurs Anyone High Audio has no filter, no undo
Recording and redistribution Anyone High A recorded voice clip is a permanent artefact
Spam / commercial solicitation Tutors, agencies Medium Degrades the room and the brand
Ban evasion Repeat offenders Medium Device-level enforcement required

Design position: we cannot detect most of this in real time. We can make it rare (verification, age gate, no identifiers), make it reported in one tap, make the consequence fast and permanent, and make evidence available without recording everyone by default.

2. Age gate: 18+ at launch

  • Hard block for under-18 on the age screen — not a soft "are you sure".
  • Store birth year only, not full date of birth. Re-verify on any hint of a minor (voice, self-description, report).
  • 16–17 is a separate product (school-verified cohorts, guardian consent, same-age matching only, no 1:1 with adults) and ships only after an external child-safety review. It is not a toggle.
  • Any user who reports being a minor is immediately removed from matching pending review, regardless of their stated age.

Why this is a differentiator, not a limitation: HelloTalk's 13+ rating is one of its most-cited problems in reviews and press. Launching adult-only is a credible, defensible trust claim — and it is the only version of this product that can pass an app-store review without a child-safety programme.

3. Verification

Level What it takes What it unlocks
Unverified Email or Apple/Google sign-in Nothing — cannot enter a live room
Verified Phone number + selfie liveness check (vendor: Persona/Onfido, ~$0.60–1.20 per check, H) 1:1 and table matching
Trusted 10 completed rooms, 0 upheld reports, 30 days May open a table; lighter re-verification
  • Verification happens after the first room, before the second. The first room is with a verified user only, and the new user is flagged as new to their partner ("New here — be kind").
  • Display name is chosen by the user; real names, photos, locations, and links are not collected at all. No field exists to put them in. This is stronger than a filter.
  • One verified identity per device family. Device fingerprint + phone hash for ban enforcement; both stored hashed, deleted after 12 months of inactivity.
  • Verification is not a badge to show off — there is no visible "verified" trophy in the room. It is a gate, not a status.

Cost note: verification is the largest per-user fixed cost (~$0.90, H). At 100k verified users that is ~$90k. It is the price of the product existing, and it belongs in the unit economics (05 §5), not in a hidden growth budget.

4. Pre-call and in-call controls

Control Behaviour
No identifiers No surname, no photo, no city, no employer, no social handle. Accent is unavoidable; biography is not.
Topic-bound rooms Rooms open with a topic prompt; off-topic escalation is a reportable signal, not a script to police
One-tap report Ends the call instantly for the reporter, opens 6 reason chips, optionally records a 20-second voice note
Block Permanent, device-level, both directions, no notification to the blocked party
Mute Local mute is instant; the other side sees "muted", not the reason
Recording indicator If either side records (see §5), both see a persistent amber bar and the room cannot start without acknowledgement
Leave Always available, never confirmed, ends the session for both with a neutral "Call ended"

Report copy — exact:

What happened? Sexual or inappropriate · Harassment or threats · Hate speech · They seem under 18 · Scam or spam · Something else

Optional: hold to record 20 seconds.

Submit · Cancel

One screen. No "are you sure", no "this will notify the other person".

5. Recording and evidence: the hard trade-off

Default: no recording, no server-side audio retention. Audio is relayed, not stored. This is a real promise and it is in the privacy policy in one sentence.

But a report with no evidence is a he-said-she-said queue, and that is how harassment goes unpunished. The resolution:

Rolling 60-second encrypted buffer, device-local, never uploaded unless a report is filed within five minutes.

Property Spec
Where On-device only (iOS/Android encrypted file, app-private)
Length 60 seconds, overwritten continuously
Upload Only on report, only the 60s preceding the tap
Retention 30 days if a report is filed; otherwise overwritten within 60s
Disclosure A one-line notice at room start: "For safety, the last minute is kept on your device and only shared if either of you reports." + a "How this works" link
Who can access Trust & Safety reviewers, logged, two-person approval for playback
User access Either party can request their own clip under GDPR/CCPA

This is disclosed, symmetric, and limited. It does not make the product "recorded"; it makes reports adjudicable. Users who decline the buffer can still use the app, but their reports are "no evidence" and enforcement is limited to pattern-of-behaviour analysis (which is weaker — and the UI says so).

6. Enforcement ladder

Step Trigger Action Appeal
1 First upheld minor report 24h suspension + mandatory policy read Yes
2 Sexual harassment, threats, hate speech 7-day suspension, removed from matching Yes, human review
3 Second offence, or any physical threat Permanent ban, device-level Yes, one review
4 Scam/romance fraud pattern Permanent ban, payment processor notified No
5 CSAM, credible threat to life Permanent ban, preservation of evidence, report to NCMEC / law enforcement No
  • Target time-to-action: 24 hours for sexual/harassment reports, 4 hours for minor-safety, immediate automated mute on report submission. These are commitments, not aspirations; they appear in the launch checklist (06 §4).
  • Moderation staffing: 2 FTE Trust & Safety at launch, 24/5 coverage, on-call escalation for minor-safety. Ratio target: 1 reviewer per 25k monthly active users (H; measure queue depth, not ratio).
  • Reviewer wellbeing: rotating exposure limits, counselling budget, no reviewer sees the same case twice.

7. Moderation stack, by layer

Layer Mechanism Reality check
Signup Phone + liveness, device fingerprint, deny-list Catches ban evasion, not intent
Pre-room Topic prompts, age gate, no identifiers Reduces surface area
In-room On-device keyword spotting → local warning ("that word isn't okay here") Never uploads audio; high false-negative rate; do not over-claim
In-room One-tap report, instant end, evidence buffer The only mechanism that reliably works
Post-room Human review queue sorted by severity, repeat-offender graph Expensive; the bottleneck
Structural No DMs, no follow, no profile browsing, 6-person cap The most effective control in the whole system

What we will not claim: that we detect abuse in real time, that AI moderation makes rooms safe, or that verification proves someone is safe. Verification proves accountability, not character.

8. Metrics and launch gates

Metric Definition Launch gate (H)
Report rate Reports per 1,000 room-minutes ≤ 3.0 and falling
Upheld rate Upheld / reviewed Track only; > 60% means over-reporting, < 25% means under-action
Repeat offenders Users with ≥ 2 upheld reports < 5% of reported users
Time to action Report → enforcement P50 ≤ 12h, P95 ≤ 24h
Minor exposure Rooms with a suspected minor Zero tolerance; any confirmed case freezes matching
Coverage Room-minutes by a verified participant ≥ 99%
Appeals overturned Appeals upheld / appeals < 15%

Kill signal: if report rate does not decline over two consecutive months while verification and moderation are fully staffed, the matching model is the problem, not the moderation. Stop growth and redesign matching (e.g., reputation-gated pairing).

9. What we tell users, exactly

Three sentences, on the safety screen and the App Store description:

Everyone you meet is verified as an adult. We never share your location, your full name, or your contacts. One tap ends any call and sends it to a human reviewer.

If any of those becomes untrue, the copy changes before the feature ships — not after.
